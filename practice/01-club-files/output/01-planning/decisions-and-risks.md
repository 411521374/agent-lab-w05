# Decisions And Risks

## Decision Log

| Decision | Current state | Evidence | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indoor or outdoor activity | Not decided | `meeting_notes.txt` | Decide at next meeting. |
| Outdoor 30-minute proposal | Draft only | `proposal_final.txt` | Compare with indoor option. |
| Indoor 20-minute proposal | Draft only | `proposal_final2.txt` | Compare with outdoor option. |
| Time and location | Not decided | `announcement.txt` | Request confirmed details. |
| Paper budget | Not approved | `budget_draft.txt` | Request budget approval. |

## Risk Log

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Rain affects outdoor activity | Outdoor proposal may not work. | Discuss indoor alternative before approving outdoor plan. |
| Announcement lacks time and location | Members may receive incomplete information. | Keep announcement as draft until details are confirmed. |
| Duplicate files cause mistaken deletion | Evidence could be lost. | Preserve all originals and document duplicate-looking pairs. |
| Filename suggests false approval | `final2` may be mistaken for approved plan. | Treat both proposals as drafts until a human approves one. |
